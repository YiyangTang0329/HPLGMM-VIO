## Materials for Review

To facilitate the review process, all relevant materials have been made publicly available in this repository. The files are organized as follows:

- **`Condensed_Manuscript_No_Color.pdf`**  
  The clean version of the revised manuscript prepared in compliance with the 20-page limit of *IEEE Transactions on Robotics*, without color-coded revision marks.

- **`Condensed_Manuscript_with_Color.pdf`**  
  The marked-up version of the 20-page revised manuscript, in which the changes made during the current revision round are highlighted in color.

- **`Full_Manuscript_No_Color.pdf`**  
  The clean and complete version of the manuscript, incorporating all mathematical proofs, simulation studies, experimental results, and supporting analyses, without revision marks.

- **`Full_Manuscript_with_Color.pdf`**  
  The marked-up complete version of the manuscript, with the revisions made during the current round highlighted in color.

- **`Supplementary_Materials_No_Color.pdf`**  
  The clean version of the supplementary material, including additional mathematical proofs, simulation validation, ablation studies, parameter-sensitivity analyses, consistency analyses, and other experimental evaluations supporting the conclusions of the manuscript.

- **`Supplementary_Materials_with_Color.pdf`**  
  The marked-up version of the supplementary material, with newly added or revised content highlighted in color.

The condensed manuscript is the formal version submitted for review, whereas the full manuscript and supplementary material are provided to facilitate a more complete assessment of the theoretical derivations and experimental validation.
